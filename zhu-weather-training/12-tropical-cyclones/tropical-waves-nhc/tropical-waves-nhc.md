What are tropical waves?
* Perturbations / disturbances in the tropical easterlies that 
typically move from east to west.
* Often seen as inverted troughs of low pressure (inverted-V 
pattern in satellite imagery). Significant rain producers.
* Convection typically on the east side. Subsidence/clearing 
on the west side.  
* Convection highly modulated by atmospheric moisture, 
upper level features, topography, etc.
* Develop into tropical cyclones.
* Around 60 tracked per year (little annual variability)
