LOW CLOUD FORECASTING QUIZ
1)
What are the 2 main factors required for the formation of low 
cloud?
ANS:  Low level moisture and turbulence (wind shear)
2)
What is the relationship between the height of the base of 
stratus, the air temperature and the dewpoint?
ANS:  Hst = (TSt-Tdew)x350
3)
What are the 3 primary ways of clearing stratus?
ANS:  Increasing wind; drier air; insolation
4)
What 2 features on a tephigram can mark the top of a layer of 
cloud?
ANS:  Inversion; isothermal.
