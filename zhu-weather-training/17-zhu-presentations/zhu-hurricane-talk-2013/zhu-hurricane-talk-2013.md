## Page 26

![Page 26](pages/page-026.jpg)

Hurricane Outlook for 2013 
• Tropical Atlantic has warmed over the past several 
months which is necessary for tropical formation.  
SST’s of 80deg F or greater 
 
• El Nino unlikely which would generally indicated a 
less active tropical season 
 
• Since 1900, there were 5 years with similar patterns 
to February/March 2013…and 4 out of 5 had above 
normal tropical activity

• We remain in a warm multi-decadal 
oscillation 
• The MDO does not affect the number of 
storms and weak hurricanes 
• It noticeably increases the number of weak 
hurricanes that mature and become major 
hurricanes
